Sonance Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Sonance is a chromatic instrument tuner by ImagineBowl. Your privacy matters to us.

Microphone

Sonance uses your device microphone to detect pitch in real time for tuning. Audio is processed on your device only. We do not record, store, or transmit audio.

Data collection

We do not collect personal information. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Local storage

Your input sensitivity preference is saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties.
