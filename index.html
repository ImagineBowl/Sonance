Sonance Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Sonance is a chromatic instrument tuner and metronome by ImagineBowl. Your privacy matters to us.

Microphone

The tuner uses your device microphone to detect pitch in real time. Audio is processed on your device only. We do not record, store, or transmit audio from the microphone.

Audio output

The metronome plays click sounds through your device speaker or headphones. These sounds are generated on your device. The metronome does not use the microphone.

Data collection

We do not collect personal information. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Local storage

App preferences — such as tuner sensitivity, instrument mode, metronome tempo and time signature, and dismissed update prompts — are saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage.

Network

Sonance may check Apple’s App Store for available app updates. Only your app’s bundle identifier is sent to Apple’s public lookup service. No personal information is included.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties.
